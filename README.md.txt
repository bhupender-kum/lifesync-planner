⚡ LifeSync OS — High-Performance Discipline, Schedule & Wealth Engine

LifeSync OS is a production-grade, single-file daily & weekly planner, time-blocking matrix, habit tracker, and rupee expense vault designed to help you execute your days with absolute focus and clarity.

🌟 Key Features

🎯 1. Focus Arena (TO DO LIST & Anti-Goals)

Non-Negotiable Priorities: High-impact daily action items with fast checkmarks and completion streaks.

Anti-Goal Guardrails: Explicit rules against energy drains, doomscrolling, and self-sabotaging habits.

⏱️ 2. Action Blueprint & Time-Blocking

3-Zone Focus View: Structured into 🌅 Morning Ignition (06:00 – 12:00), ☀️ Afternoon Execution (12:00 – 18:00), and 🌙 Evening Mastery (18:00 – 24:00).

1-Hour Time Grid Breakdown: Hour-by-hour precision mapping with category tags (Work, Health, Study, Career, Personal).

Current Hour Pulse: Real-time pulse ring highlighting the active hour block.

🦊 3. Interactive Cartoon Companion ("Kiko Fox")

Reactive vector mascot featuring animated expressions, tail wags, ear wiggles, and mood updates based on your daily progress score.

Dynamic comic speech bubbles providing context-aware motivation and anti-distraction reminders.

💰 4. Rupee Ledger & Monthly Vault (₹ INR)

Quick expense logging categorized by Food & Chai, Travel, Bills, Shopping, Learning, and Health.

Dual categorization for Need vs. Want to maintain financial discipline.

Real-time monthly spending progress bar and safety buffer calculators.

📲 5. Mobile & Calendar Integration

1-Tap Google Calendar Sync: Push individual focus blocks directly into Google Calendar to trigger sound alarms on your phone.

WhatsApp Agenda Dispatch: Pre-formatted WhatsApp summary export for self-accountability.

Universal .ics Calendar Export: Download full-day schedules compatible with iOS, Android, and Outlook.

📊 6. Analytics & 30-Day Heatmap

Pillar Allocation Donut Chart: Interactive visualization of time spent across Work, Health, Study, Career, and Leisure.

Planned vs. Actual Spend Bar Chart: Weekly expense comparison against daily target budgets.

30-Day Discipline Heatmap Grid: GitHub-style consistency matrix tracking habit retention over time.

🔒 Security & Client-Side Privacy

Zero-Leakage Local Storage: All account credentials, time blocks, and financial records reside exclusively inside the user's browser localStorage.

SHA-256 Tokenization: Client-side passphrase hashing ensures user profile isolation.

Persistent Sessions: Auto-login token validation keeps users signed in seamlessly across browser refreshes and tab closures.
